# Review workflow: quality first, total price second

The primary comparison is **the quality of the whole review package and its total
price**. Latency is secondary. A slower workspace package can be the better
choice if it preserves the needed evidence and costs less. Earlier emphasis on
the absence of a speed win understated that possibility. Keep the rule-specific
candidate and useful mixed results; do not require a universal engine winner.

This reassessment uses saved AI, AL, AO and AX outcomes, plus the AR evidence-map
checks and AY examples. AZ reconciles usage for 100 historical calls across the
four priced studies without dispatching another model or changing the frozen
`a0feb12` implementation. These are distinct development comparisons, not a pooled
quality score, complete project-spend ledger or independent held-out evaluation.

## Quality: what we can and cannot conclude

Judge each selected question by whether its evidence is sufficient, rather than
rewarding more source reads automatically. An additional read is valuable when
it can change the selected conclusion. A credential-engine audit is not mandatory
for every local claim; an empty-field permission helper may be decisive for a
particular deletion claim. Define that boundary before declaring the cheaper
package equivalent or the deeper package better.

The useful quality dimensions are separate:

- **Supported mechanisms retained:** does the result preserve the real defect and
  relevant effect? Existing audits retain SQL grammar injection, post-escape hooks,
  authorization mismatches and conditional cookie mechanisms. This is development
  evidence, not measured whole-app recall.
- **Justified negatives:** does source establish the selected invariant and its
  caller applicability? Closing a card-ownership claim must not certify payment
  settlement; an inherited guard must cover the exact action/keys/fields before
  the effect. A guard name alone or a nearby citation is insufficient.
- **Useful uncertainty:** an unavailable external sanitizer contract is an honest
  outcome. An available local helper left unread after exhaustion is different:
  it preserves a useful question, but also leaves avoidable completion work. Do
  not score all `needs_review` alike or reward a forced dismissal.
- **Calibrated claims:** conditions, API inferences and severity must match the
  inspected evidence. Terra's additional conditional labels and AL's high/medium/
  high hook severities are not agreement on deployed impact or risk ranking.
- **Reviewer usability:** can a capable reviewer follow the selected caller,
  check, effect, qualifications and next question without reconstructing the app?
  AR's addressed source fixes four of four selected caller sets versus one of four
  raw sets. AY makes separate local/external outcomes and workflow leads visible.
  Human review time saved has not been measured.

The existing implementation-agent audits have not demonstrated a wrong safe
dismissal in the selected recent packages. That is narrower than proving quality
equivalence: some proof is missing, some conclusions rely on ordinary API contracts,
and independent mixed-quality adjudication remains absent. Extra evaluator reads
cannot be credited as evidence the reviewer model had.

Workspace has concrete quality value: conditional facts, independent applicability,
local partial closure and preservation of adjacent leads. Native also shares work
within its normal bundles and sometimes establishes a stronger caller/authority
chain. Neither architecture owns all of those benefits. Keep useful packages with
explicit imperfections rather than discard them for failing an identical-depth
or universal speed test.

## Total price: what is still missing

The full price for the stated review package includes:

```text
planning and source preparation
+ all reviewer calls, continuations, failures and actual cache charges
+ lookup/controller/assembly compute
+ adjudication, correction and follow-up needed to make the package usable
```

A model-side planning pass must be counted if used. Manual selection and source
preparation are currently assisted and incompletely timed; they are not free or
automatically amortized. Shared study preparation is not assigned wholly to either
arm. Infrastructure and human unit costs are also unrecorded. Therefore we can
compare measured model-price equivalents, but **cannot yet claim lower total
production price**. Do not invent a wage or charge wall-clock waiting as human
labor. If the intended metric is provider spend only, label it explicitly and
still include all model-assisted preparation, rejected calls and paid repairs.

Compare the cost of the same requested package at a stated evidence boundary.
Cost per issue alone rewards overclassification and ignores clean controls;
cost per closed claim rewards unsupported negatives. Neither is an adequate
primary metric. Preserve the quality dimensions alongside price rather than
average errors, uncertainty and useful findings into one arbitrary score.

## Revised practical decision and next evidence

1. **Retain the selective workspace direction on quality/price grounds.** Sol's
   slower historical packages are often cheaper at the model layer and preserve
   useful mechanisms/state. For prepared inherited relationships AX is especially
   promising. Keep native local SQL/parser/API closure, where ordinary batching
   already shares deciding work and no extra workspace quality benefit is shown.
2. **Spend the next evaluation effort on consequential quality gaps and total
   cost accounting.** Define the selected proof boundary, then adjudicate a small
   mixed set with supported positives, clean controls and genuinely unavailable
   facts. Existing weak spots include omitted authority-consumer proof, exact
   permission-filter behavior, post-control transformations and calibrated impact.
   Keep source delivery identical where comparing engines, and record preparation
   and follow-up costs. These are distinct deciding questions, not another speed
   tournament or permission to resume retired studies.

Prefer the lower-price package when both are sufficient for the stated reviewer
task; accept useful scoped imperfections explicitly. Narrow an approach if it
loses a supported mechanism, hides a critical assumption or forces a wrong
dismissal. If higher price buys a material quality benefit, retain that option.
Speed remains operational information or a stated latency constraint, not a
reason by itself to reject a cheaper, adequate result. Features stay frozen and
no new paid run or default promotion follows automatically from this reassessment.

Extra relevant input can be a good purchase: supply the specific missing caller,
permission transformation or authority edge when it improves the selected proof.
Count its price, but do not shorten qualifications or withhold deciding source to
meet a prompt-size target. A first-response decision is valuable only when its
evidence boundary is appropriate.

## After the complete BG comparison

The [full four-package comparison](experiments/review-full-hybrid-comparison-results.md)
now supplies 116 outcomes per candidate and actual whole-package receipts.
Terra hybrid is at +0.70% to stored native Terra; Sol's known cost is already
+1.68% to native Sol before one unmeasured timeout. The earlier focused prices
cannot substitute for these totals. Full production price remains unmeasured.

The broader source audit also qualifies earlier limited observations about wrong
safe dismissals: both Sol packages' like-review same-record reasoning misses a
version-bound selection-order counterexample, and local YAML content has a
concrete conditional writer/effect chain. Some Terra broad authorization negatives
assume a stronger verifier contract than the declared dependency establishes.
Conversely, Terra retains useful questions while leaving some available local
producers unread, and loses a distinct unpaid-entitlement lead in its hybrid
package. New Sol adds supported conditional frontend chains and a generated
product-update mechanism. Neither verdict totals nor greater caution establish a
uniform quality winner.

Keep native local closure and selective workspace state. Native peer variation
is not a workspace-routing effect. The affected 15 claims account for only
11–12% of native price, so substantial overall improvement needs to reach the
remaining native continuation work. Prioritize exact versioned facts with
provenance and applicability, plus mechanism-preserving package completion.
Freeze further paid full-app repetition until a concrete correction answers a
new small-slice quality/price question. Preserve useful mixed results and precise
uncertainty; do not buy another run merely to make the table look cleaner.
