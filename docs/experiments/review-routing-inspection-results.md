## Useful cost warning

The proposed hybrid adds **two execution units** to this particular native
partition. Basket joins claims from two original buckets; deluxe's members already
fit the same resource-access bucket. Native fragments remain in both buckets,
so the two workspace investigations do not eliminate their original packets.
The policy pair replaces its entire native packet with one workspace investigation.

The inspector now exposes this fragmentation, along with groups already contained
in one native packet. Do not claim fewer calls from seven workspace claims or
three investigations. Native packet membership is not proof of perfect semantic
reuse either; a measured mixed execution must establish the benefit of retained
facts, partial closure or shared deciding work after paying for extra contexts.
This cost warning narrows the experiment; it does not discard the supported
deluxe mechanism or policy-state behavior.

For the next dispatcher, preserve original-bundle aggregate allowances across
fragments. Basket connects two original buckets, so its shared investigation,
deluxe and their remaining native fragments need one connected-component ledger.
Giving all four units independent full budgets would compare more allowed work
against the original two native buckets. Freeze the combined allowances before
any paid run, count shared calls once and reassemble all original claim IDs.
