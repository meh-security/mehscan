Evaluator criteria are frozen separately and never supplied to providers. Audit:

- The selected controller branch, accepted request shape, selector and concrete
  service accountability/schema, rather than an operation name or response alone.
- Concrete-class batch fork and single/query-to-many dispatch, including each
  applicable override. Shared implementation is not identical update/delete policy.
- The exact action, keys and fields validated before the selected effect, including
  the relevant hook/payload transformations, parent item check and transaction order.
- Effective rejection and item/field permission behavior, with source evidence,
  API inference and deployment/configuration conditions distinguished explicitly.
  A guard name alone is insufficient; reading the entire application is not required.
- Separate source-supported scoped conclusions and genuinely decisive missing
  facts. Do not force every missing deployed policy into either a safe result or
  an issue, and do not replace the selected invariant with a stronger exploit goal.
- Correct caller/check/effect source citations and retained conditions, distinct
  leads and actionable uncertainty. Label agreement, fewer queries or smaller
  prompts alone are not quality or efficiency acceptance.

Unknown usage, source/runtime/configuration drift or an evident repeated-retrieval
loop without a new deciding question stops further dispatch. A known-cost
independent-arm failure is preserved; continuing other independent arms requires
an explicit local audit that identifies the boundary and verifies no shared drift
or unknown usage. Never repair or resume that failed arm. Cap-censored work is a
partial lower bound, not an equal completed efficiency baseline.
