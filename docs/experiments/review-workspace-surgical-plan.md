Keep a case/component ledger with source binding, supported mechanism, missing
qualification, intervention and actual cost. Preserve X's original whole-package
status and report new targeted evidence beside it. Do not retroactively call the
combined sequence a clean four-case trial or compute whole-package savings from

For a filter-only correction, deterministic cross-tool/state checks plus the
affected live case are the relevant regression scope. If a later change alters
HTML guidance or semantic closure, add a small contrast set: direct safe control,
materially unsafe shared hook and unknown binding. Preserve independently supported
SQL/authorization results; rerun them only when the changed behavior could affect
them. Source changes, shared-proposition revisions or changed caller assumptions
do require reconsideration of their actual consumers.

After the affected gap is resolved, a fresh representative mixed sample may test
generalization and incremental work. It need not repeat every historical success.
Continue to retain genuine unresolved cases and supported findings. Larger studies
are warranted when remaining uncertainty concerns coverage or cross-rule behavior;
they are not needed merely to repair one filter or finish one proof. Full-app
dispatch and the prepared main/reserved waves remain separate decisions.

Stop adding machinery when an existing question/source record expresses the gap,
or when repeated changes only increase bookkeeping. Stop local inference at the
frozen bound and retain useful partial evidence. A final verdict supported by its
actual mechanism, scoped negative or actionable uncertainty remains valuable even
when another case is incomplete.
