The source audit retains SQL grammar injection, including login's TOTP

Ownership negatives bind the middleware-established identity to the actual
selected resource/effect. They do not certify payment safety. File/YAML negatives
use the filename-prefix prerequisite and fixed metadata input; they do not
certify the YAML loader for hostile documents or claim that `existsSync` is a

Basket remains unresolved at the generated updater's ownership-to-write
relationship. Both arms inspect application middleware/models and tests but
lack the dependency implementation. Generated Product review likewise identifies
the missing PUT guard but declines to infer persistence from a response assertion
alone. Grouping did not make either missing fact available. Three `needs_review`
dispositions per arm are useful gaps, not three established findings or negatives.

### A retained lead is not a retained finding

Both arms retain two distinct adjacent concerns: deluxe membership without the
payment prerequisite, and wallet credit amount/payment settlement. Singletons
emit four raw leads because the same deluxe concern appears on three claims;
the grouped package emits two raw leads, with the deluxe concern recorded once.
This reduces duplicated reviewer follow-up without dropping that concern.

However, `source_leads` become **unvalidated `reviewer_origin_leads`**, separate
from the selected verdict; see the [native conversion](../../crates/cli/src/main.rs)
and [review completion contract](../../skills/mehscan-security/references/finish-review.md).
They are not classified findings. The earlier expanded report's statement that
the payment mechanism survives as a lead must not be read as evidence that its
finding status survives. This is a workflow gap shared by both fresh arms, not
proof that all supported operation findings have been preserved.

The next contract should keep original claim verdicts separate from independently
assessed operation mechanisms, with explicit status, conditions, diagnosis and
evidence. A supported adjacent mechanism should not have to become an open
question merely because the selected scanner claim concerns ownership. Never
automatically promote free-text lead content into a finding.

## Corrected direction

1. **Proceed with concrete rule-specific groups.** SQL construction batches and
   compatible same-operation authorization reviews have evidence behind them.
   Preserve separate verdicts and partial closure. Do not automatically merge
   by file/category or treat a capped unresolved investigation as a proved efficiency win.
2. **Provide shared deciding dependencies once.** Record snapshot-scoped source
   unavailability and distinguish declared dependency range, resolved version,
   reference source and observed runtime effect. A version/provenance-bound
   generated-CRUD contract can serve basket and Product without transferring
   an authorization verdict. Prefer relevant cached raw guard/effect definitions
   over making the model request the same short helper in every new context.
3. **Separate operation mechanisms from scanner closure.** Preserve supported
   adjacent mechanisms as explicitly assessed records, and unresolved leads as
   leads. Finding/lead equivalence and severity remain review-package issues.
4. **Expose this state in a small interactive CLI**, including claims/work,
   deciding question, requested fact, receipts, missing dependency, mechanism
   status, fact consumers and resume/split actions. Inspection makes no model call.
5. **Test the compact AI grouping pass and a held-out mixed slice.** The current
   groups were deterministic. Include genuine bound-query controls, dynamic
   fragments and incompatible caller policies before generalizing.

No further repeat of this pair is scheduled. A single development pair supports
the component direction, not a statistical whole-app saving. No full-app rerun
until supported mechanisms, uncertainty handling and reviewer-visible finding
status pass the broader quality check. Larger useful inputs and modest output
remain acceptable; old token-percentage gates stay retired.
