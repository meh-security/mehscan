found that the workflow display driver sanitizes the configured Summary

do not establish every summary writer's authority or effective sanitizer
attribute policy. The final question remains the writer/recipient relationship
and whether qualifying event-target attributes can survive the applicable path.

## Timing anomaly and correction for the wider study

Both groups completed their calls and native exports within their original
deadlines: the client group reports **84.463 seconds**, notifications **159.574
seconds**. The last group-completion event is 159.589 seconds after stage start.
However, the completion summary was written **16,538.120 seconds** after start,
with a **16,378.531-second gap after group completion**. The combined recorded
native-plus-verifier stage time is therefore 17,087.107 seconds. Do not silently
replace that wall time with the roughly 160-second review phase or claim an
end-to-end speed improvement.

Windows logged Modern Standby transitions during the gap. The dispatched runner
released its execution request before final frozen-file verification. That is
consistent with a post-review standby pause, though the exact allocation of the
whole gap was not instrumented. All paid calls and group exports preceded it;
no additional inference or budget restart occurred. `power-events.json` and
`timing-audit.json` retain the evidence.

The wider runner holds the execution request through final verification and
completion publication, and records review completion and final verification
separately. This is a concrete runner correction; the original dispatched script
and timing record remain unchanged. It does not prevent an explicit manual
sleep/power action or prove the wider run's eventual timing in advance.

## Decision and larger scope

Keep both modes: cached native for local contracts and compatible claims, and
shared stateful investigations where a material producer/authority relationship
remains. The new producer/control evidence and preserved questions make a useful
review package even when no finding is added. Avoid turning the price increase
in this tiny targeted slice into a general rejection of workspace or a blanket
requirement for a second pass.

The user's request to broaden scope supersedes the previous small-slice stop.
Proceed with **120 held-out claims across 105 files, 26 rules and 13 capability
families**, excluding all 30 calibration claims: 112 reviewed-Value selections
and eight explicit Comprehensive controls. Freeze current guidance, reuse exact
known helper source where its implementation matches, and measure actual
broader cost/cache/time and quality. Independent failures remain explicit while
unaffected work continues. No full-app extrapolation or automatic admission is
claimed from this six-claim result.
