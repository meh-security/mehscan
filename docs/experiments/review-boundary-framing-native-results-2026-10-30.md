### Why six negatives do not settle their complete questions

**Notifications:** the model correctly observes that the static wrapper elements
do not set `data-target-url`, and preserves a separate raw-summary concern. But
`Notification.Header.cshtml:20–25` and `Notification.SummaryAdmin.cshtml:33–40`
render `notification.Summary` as raw HTML inside the listener subtree. The
handler uses `e.target`, reads its gating attributes at lines 35–51, and reads
the destination at lines 93–97. The static wrapper does not settle the attributes
or authority of a dynamic descendant. No malicious summary is proved; the
producer/sanitization and selected DOM relationship remain decisive. Treating
that relationship only as an adjacent HTML lead loses part of this claim's input.

**Configured client:** the method arguments are escaped into query values or
serialized as bodies and do not themselves change the authority. Keep that
useful local closure. The native question explicitly also asks about intended
authority, effective destination and credential/data disclosure. The actual
receiver is **ElasticsearchApiClient**, not an arbitrary same-named helper in
the generated file. Its constructor accepts an HttpClient and defaults BaseUrl
to `/` (`OpenApiClient.cs:803–810`); its BaseUrl setter accepts configuration
(`818–826`), and requests invoke preparation hooks before SendAsync. The inspected
package does not establish the selected consumer/configuration authority.
Neither SSRF nor an affirmatively safe full destination relationship follows
from the local scalar/body fact. Partial-hook declarations alone are not proof
that a rewrite implementation exists; the concrete configuration gap is enough
to preserve uncertainty without inventing an active hook.

These are **six insufficient full-scope closures in two shared relationships**,
not six confirmed missed vulnerabilities. The audit changes only derived
diagnostic drafts to `needs_review`, retains the paid output unchanged, and
validates every derived draft/report through the ordinary native finalizer.
It adds no safe verdict, invented attacker, application finding or native
admission. The manual semantic work is not priced as zero-cost autonomy.
