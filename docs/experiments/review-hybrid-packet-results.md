extra workflow leads. Do not promote uniform hybrid review as faster. Correct
the known wrapper-source boundary in fresh actual packets first.

does not establish the remaining context-sharing opportunity: a genuinely related
registration/check/effect investigation split across different native rule buckets.

needs a concrete compatible relationship, separate verdicts, no stranded peer
fragments, counted preparation and equal aggregate allowance. If those conditions
do not hold, stop that variant rather than repeat already-one-call packets. Fewer
containers alone still do not predict saved calls or establish memory reuse.

to exact identity/coverage/freshness checks. They cannot establish model savings
or explain the larger model-time gap by themselves.
