# Review workflow: current evidence and decisions

The practical direction is a hybrid selected by deciding work, with packet
placement chosen separately from logical investigation. **Quality and total price
are primary; latency is secondary.** No whole-app quality or total-price win has
been established.

are 22.4% lower in AI, 8.3% in AL and 23.8% in AO, despite slower observed execution.

AX Sol is censored and not an equal-closure saving. These use current published

remain, but caller/authority proof, severity and preparation/follow-up costs need
explicit evaluation. Keep the selective candidate; do not equate slower with worse
or matching labels with equal quality.

Native is the practical initial choice for complete SQL constructions, cookie
flags and local parser/API decisions. This is not a proven SQL engine ranking.
Workspace remains appropriate for selected shared dependencies, different caller
conditions, check-to-effect relationships and partial closure. Co-location does
not turn a wallet selector into the deluxe operation or an external policy into
the inspected local sanitizer.

Source/profile selection is still manually assisted. Preparation, planning,
provider calls, output, actual model/active time and assembly all count. Input
reduction, shorter responses, fewer groups and lower configured ceilings are not
success criteria. Saved implementation-agent audits are not independent human gold.
