## Quality assessment of the whole package

Each candidate must account for every original ID, including incomplete and
cap-censored work. Keep original claim results and group related mechanisms only
for evaluator comparison; multiple scanner markers are not automatically multiple
unique vulnerabilities. Native validation and physical excerpts do not certify
semantic correctness.

Freeze the selected evidence boundary and evaluator criteria before dispatch.
Audit supported mechanisms, justified negatives, caller/check/effect relevance,
explicit API/deployment assumptions, severity and actionable uncertainty. Do not
require identical proof depth or an exhaustive application investigation when a
local invariant is complete. Distinguish an unavailable dependency from an unread
available helper, and scoped local closure from whole-operation safety.

Cover known complex mechanisms and controls without forcing old labels: SQL and
its login qualification, post-control/selector mismatches, cookie request/delivery
conditions, inherited/generated authority, fixed redirects and scoped ownership.
Retain new adjacent leads separately from classified findings. Juice does not
contain an admitted safe parameterized SQL control or the constructed unavailable
external sanitizer control; do not manufacture those claims or present earlier
fixture tests as newly validated app outcomes.

Adjudicate all consequential disagreements with workflow/model labels masked
where practical. Include deterministic, rule-stratified source checks of agreeing
negatives so consensus mistakes are not invisible. Freeze that sampling method
before reading outcomes. Keep evaluator-only reads separate from provider evidence.
Without a separate independent reviewer, label this implementation-agent
adjudication; a fresh provider session is not independent gold.

Report quality by family and the price of the complete requested package. Keep
several modes when one is cheaper while another supplies material evidence or
better uncertainty/calibration. A lower-price package is preferred when both are
sufficient for the stated reviewer task; useful scoped imperfections do not
require an all-or-nothing rejection. Wrong dismissals and lost mechanisms need
specific diagnosis, not a cheaper-looking aggregate score.
